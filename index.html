ABOUT

A small studio, built for close work.

Habakkuk Designs is a South African digital studio led by William, working with founders and businesses who want their digital presence to actually look like the quality of what they do.

We keep engagements small and direct on purpose — one point of contact, clear timelines, and no hand-off to a stranger halfway through the project. Every brief goes through the same process: understand the business, design with intent, build it properly, and stay reachable after launch.

  • 01 Discovery & strategy — understand the goal before touching a screen.
  • 02 Design & prototyping — a visual direction built for your audience, not a template.
  • 03 Build & launch — clean, fast, responsive, and tested before it ships.
  • 04 Support — ongoing care once you're live.
